where does this link go

Short links and email wrappers hide their destination. One request follows the redirects (the shortener counts a click), then the final address is checked against the lookalike list.

Redirect chain of the current tab

Every hop the browser took to reach the page you came from.

    QR code check

    Decode a QR code from a picture or a screenshot before you open it. The link is checked like any other; nothing is uploaded.

    read this policy

    Open a privacy policy or terms page in a tab, then press the button: the shield reads the page and says in a few lines what it actually commits you to.

    forget me

    A deletion request under GDPR or CCPA, ready to send. Fill it in, copy it, and mail it to the company's privacy address (usually privacy@ or dpo@ their domain).

    People-search and data-broker opt-outs

    Each of these publishes or sells profiles. Their own removal pages, in one list. Work through them; most take a few minutes each.

      Receipts and warranties

      Order confirmations the shield saw, with what you spent per store per month. Add a warranty length and it reminds you two weeks before it ends.
