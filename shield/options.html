vpn locations

The VPN sends everything the browser does through one location you choose, so sites see that location's address instead of yours. It fails closed: if the location stops answering, requests stop instead of slipping out on your own connection. Names are looked up by the location and WebRTC calls cannot reveal your real address. It protects this browser, not the rest of the computer. Locations come from noah's signed list, from servers you add here, or from Tor on this computer (the anonymous route; its exit country changes on its own).

Add a location of your own

Any HTTPS proxy or SOCKS5 server works: one you rent, one a friend runs, or Tor on this computer. A plain http proxy across the internet is refused because everyone on the path could read it.

Vetted servers come from noah's signed feed (not fetched yet).

Timezone, language and coordinates follow the chosen location, so they agree with the tunnel's exit. A page reload picks changes up.

Results as the engine ranked them, not as advertisers paid for them. Works on Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Yahoo, Startpage and Ecosia; each cleaned page shows a small count, and the log keeps what was removed.

Parental mode

Adult sites and keywords blocked, safe search forced on Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo and YouTube. Changing it needs the PIN. It is a browser-level filter, not a substitute for the operating system's family controls.

shopping

Price lookups fetch the other stores' public search pages with no cookies and only the product's name. The shield never places an order; it fills the code box and presses apply.

Other extensions in this browser

An extension with capture permissions and reach into every site can take a screenshot of every frame you see. Ones that were not installed from a store weigh more. Disabling is reversible here or in the browser's own extension page.

Programs outside the browser (screen recorders, remote access tools) are what noah's device room looks for.

shepherd persona on every AI

On every AI chat you open — Gemini, Gemini Lens, ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity, Poe, Mistral, DeepSeek, HuggingChat, Grok, Yandex Alice, Character.ai, Pi, You.com, Kagi Assistant, Venice, AI Studio — the bundled shepherd document is pasted into the composer of a fresh conversation and sent as turn one. Every response after that runs under the shepherd rules. A small pill on the AI page shows the state and carries a "reseed" button. Auto-seeding only fires on a fresh chat with an empty composer.

x profile intelligence

On any X or Twitter profile page a floating report opens with account age and post rate, the rest_id and its rough signup era, verified/protected/withheld flags, follow ratio, bio URLs expanded (X hides them behind t.co), cross-platform handles pulled out of the bio, and one-click links to reverse-image-search the profile picture, browse older versions on Wayback Machine, and check any email in the bio against HIBP. Data comes from what your browser was already going to receive; external lookups only fire when you click them.

twitter / x spaces

On an X or Twitter Space page the shield adds a small panel listing every listener with their @, name and a link to their profile. Listeners in a Space are not anonymous; their handles ride in the audio-space responses the browser already gets, but the desktop and mobile apps hide them. Data stays in this browser.

inspect mode (for stress-testing your own site)

A tab you arm from the popup records every fetch, XHR, WebSocket frame, form submit, storage read, CSP violation, and cross-origin postMessage the page sees, in plain text, with JWTs decoded and known secret shapes flagged. A snapshot button also pulls cookies, localStorage and sessionStorage. Copy-out puts the transcript on the clipboard for your security team. Off by default, per tab. Never turn it on for a site you do not own.

instagram

Flags sponsored posts, hides suggested content, surfaces algorithmic signals (Explore vs. Following, Reel rank), and adds a session-time reminder so the feed doesn't swallow hours. Reel focus mode hides the sidebar and related carousel.

tiktok

Adds a live session clock TikTok deliberately hides, flags paid promotions and unverified creators, hides suggested accounts, and shows an exit panel once you hit your session limit. Focus mode removes the sidebar discovery carousel.

reddit

Removes promoted posts, converts fuzzy "3 days ago" timestamps to exact dates, flags karma-farming title patterns, and can hide the award clutter that inflates post authority. Works on both the new (shreddit) and old Reddit.

youtube

Removes the recommendation sidebar trap while watching, cleans endscreen cards, disables autoplay, shows exact upload dates with content age, flags sponsored disclosures in descriptions, and detects clickbait title patterns. Optionally hides the Shorts tab.

linkedin

Removes sponsored content and job ads, converts "1 week ago" to a real date with days-since count, detects engagement-bait and hustle-porn formatting, flags job postings older than your threshold that are probably already filled, and cleans suggested / trending sidebar noise.

the look

A picture sets the look: its colors become the shield's pages, the new tab page and, on Firefox, the browser's own frame and toolbars (Chrome and Edge let no extension recolor theirs). The blacks take the picture's temperature, the words stay readable, and one color from the picture becomes the accent, seen only where it matters. Nothing is uploaded; the picture stays in this browser.

about

Feeds are signed with noah's ed25519 release key and refused when the signature does not match, so the website alone cannot change what the shield does.
