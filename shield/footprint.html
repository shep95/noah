what the web holds about you

Enter what someone would search for. The shield asks several search engines in many countries, without cookies and without your address in a referrer, then draws what came back as a map. Nothing here is sent to noah's servers; the requests go straight to the engines from this browser.

the map

You in the middle; around you the kinds of places that mention you; on the outside every site. Drag anything. Hover for a line about it, click for the pages and what to do. Colours are yours to change.

getting it taken down

Plainly: no button anywhere can delete what other sites hold. Only they can. What this does is put every removal form and privacy address you need in one list, filled with your details, and remember which you have filed. With noah installed, shepherd can work through the forms for you in its browser room, asking before each one is sent; captchas and "click the link in your email" steps stay yours.

other places to look yourself

Pages that cannot be searched from here (they need you signed in, or they refuse programs). Each opens in a new tab with your details in the search box.
