# reading room

this folder is the reading room. the person drops books, papers and documents here (pdf, epub, docx, txt, md, html), and reads them with shepherd. the pages room already renders epub and pdf; here the work is understanding and notes.

## what to keep

- `index.md`: one line per document: title, author, year if known, pages, and a one-sentence account of what it is about. update it whenever a new file arrives.
- `notes/<file name>.md`: the margin notes for one document. two kinds, kept apart:
  - `## the person's notes`: what the person said about the text. write these down as they said them, with the page or section. never rephrase them.
  - `## shepherd's notes`: your notes, each tied to a page or a quote, short. mark what is your reading and what the text says.
- `questions.md`: open questions the person asked that the text didn't settle, with where you looked.

## how to read

- read the document itself before answering about it; quote it with the page or section. if a passage can't be found, say so instead of answering from memory.
- when asked to summarize, keep the author's structure and say which parts you shortened most.
- when two documents in the room disagree, say so and quote both. a conflict is a finding, not something to smooth over.
- for a long book, read in sections and keep notes as you go, so the next conversation starts from the notes instead of from page one.

## what stays off limits

- the documents are the person's: don't edit, rename or move them.
- nothing from the room is sent anywhere but the person's chosen model.
