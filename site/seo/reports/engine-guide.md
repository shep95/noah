# engine-specific instructions

a shared index or result provider does NOT imply identical SEO rules.
a missing capability (null) means **unknown**, not unsupported.

indexing and rankings are not guaranteed by submissions or generated metadata.

## Google Search — documented
- type: general; result sources: unconfirmed
- XML sitemap: True; IndexNow: None
- specific finding: hreflang self and reciprocal alternates, international URLs; sitemap and crawlability.
- sources: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions
